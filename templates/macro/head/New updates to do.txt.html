1. Conceptual Deep Dive The about Subschema (The "Subject Matter") According to Schema.org, the about property describes the primary subject matter of a creative work. SEO Purpose: It informs search engines about the abstract topics, concepts,or target entities your article discusses. Best Practice: Use it to point to canonical authority records (like Wikipedia or Wikidata URLs via sameAs) so search engines know exactly which real-world concept you are discussing without ambiguity. The mainEntity Subschema (The "Primary Element") The mainEntity property specifies the principal, concrete entity that the page describes, hosts, or is a showcase for. SEO Purpose: It tells search engines, "If you strip away the headers, footers, sidebars, and comments, this specific asset is what this page is actually about. "Best Practice: For a media-focused article or video log, the embedded VideoObject is designated as the mainEntity. This makes the page eligible for rich Google Video Snippets in search results. 1. The about Property: Frequently a ListA web page or blog post often discusses multiple distinct topics or concepts. Therefore, the about property is very commonly a list of elements. The Exception: When mainEntity is a List You should only use a list for mainEntity if the page type explicitly demands a collection of equal-importance items.
